Groups WHO Disease Outbreak News reports into outbreaks and maps them by country. Case/death counts and country tags are extracted automatically from WHO's report text and may be incomplete or delayed — always verify against the linked source before using clinically. Open-source; PRs adding more sources welcome.
US state-level counts are the latest provisional week from CDC's National Notifiable Diseases Surveillance System (NNDSS), which is updated weekly and revised as reports come in — figures for the most recent week are usually incomplete and will rise. A blank state for a given disease means it isn't nationally notifiable there or data was unavailable that week, not that it had zero cases.
Brazil state-level data covers Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SRAG) only — the condition InfoGripe tracks — not a general outbreak feed. Source: InfoGripe, a partnership between Fiocruz (PROCC), FGV (EMAp), and Brazil's Ministry of Health, published weekly. The most recent epidemiological week is provisional and will revise as more reports arrive. Risk levels ("intensidade") are Fiocruz's own population-adjusted classification, not raw case counts.
This mode shows all-cause mortality, not a specific disease — it was the only Latin American national dataset found (beyond Brazil) that was both structured and genuinely current; Chile's actual notifiable-disease surveillance (ENO) is roughly a year stale due to anonymization review. Source: DEIS/MINSAL (Chile's Ministry of Health), weekly. The most recent week's death count is almost always an undercount — deaths take time to register — so the "vs. baseline" percentage for the latest week is not a reliable trend signal; treat it as provisional.
